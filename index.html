🔒 Your privacy

Your information is private to you. Everything you enter (your periods, settings and reminders) is saved only on this phone, inside this app. Mensura has no accounts and does not upload, sell or share your information, and its makers can’t see it.

  • Only someone who can open this app on your phone can see your data, so keep your phone locked.
  • If you clear your browser data or delete the app, your saved periods are erased and can’t be recovered.
  • You can delete everything yourself at any time with the button below.

These dates are estimates based on an average cycle. This app is not medical advice and should not be used to prevent pregnancy. See a health worker if your cycles are shorter than 21 or longer than 35 days, bleeding is very heavy, pain is severe, or you miss periods.
